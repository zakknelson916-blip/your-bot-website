Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Monk Bot processes information when it is used in a Discord server.

Information We May Process

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord usernames and display names
  • Server IDs and server configuration
  • Channel and role information
  • Warnings, kicks, timeouts, and bans
  • Moderation reasons and notes
  • Moderation logs
  • Messages or message-related information when needed for moderation features
  • Bot commands and command-related information

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide moderation features, maintain moderation records, enforce server rules, respond to commands, and maintain the security and reliability of the Bot.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the Bot.

Data Retention

Moderation information may be retained as necessary to provide moderation features, maintain server records, prevent abuse, or comply with applicable obligations.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the Bot. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Discord

The Bot operates through Discord's platform. Discord may independently process information according to its own policies and terms.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Updated versions will be published at this location.

Contact

For privacy questions concerning information processed by the Bot, contact the Bot's developer through the contact method provided with the Bot.
